# Goal Programmes and Daily Actions

**Status:** Proposed; planning model and detail are undecided.

## Idea

After understanding a goal, help the user map the steps required and arrange them into a realistic programme. Turn the programme into daily tasks that fit around fixed commitments and workable time. Support multiple simultaneous goals, allocating time according to the user's priorities.

Progress may need both consistent activity signals and milestones appropriate to the goal, rather than treating task completion alone as the outcome. See [Progress Signals and Goal Milestones](018-progress-signals-and-goal-milestones.md).

Present long goal programmes in phases to avoid overwhelming the user. Give short briefs for later phases and detail the current phase's steps; see [Phased Goal Journeys](019-phased-goal-journeys.md).

## Questions to resolve

- What makes a programme feel realistic to the user: time limits, weekly workload, milestones, or other constraints?
- How should a goal become milestones, projects, and daily tasks?
- Should the user approve each programme before tasks enter the schedule?
- How should the planner balance several goals and detect overload?
- How should it respond when tasks are repeatedly missed or the goal changes?
