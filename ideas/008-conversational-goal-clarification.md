# Conversational Goal Clarification

**Status:** Proposed; assistant behavior and boundaries need discussion.

## Idea

Let users describe ambitions in their own words. A chatbot asks follow-up questions to clarify what they actually want and why. For example, “I want to get good at coding” could lead to questions about what they want to build and their motivation, narrowing toward a direction such as game development or financial modelling.

## Questions to resolve

- How many questions should the assistant ask before it proposes a goal?
- Should users be able to skip the conversation and define a goal directly?
- How does the assistant check that it understood the user's intent?
- Should it suggest alternatives when the goal is too broad or unrealistic?
- How should goals involving multiple areas or uncertain outcomes be handled?
