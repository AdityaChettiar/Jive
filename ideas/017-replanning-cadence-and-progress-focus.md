# Replanning Cadence and Progress Focus

**Status:** Concern and guardrail proposed; timing and enforcement are undecided.

## Idea

Users should be able to suggest schedule changes, undo changes, return to older schedules, and create new schedules. At the same time, frequent replanning can feel like progress without producing progress: the user may get satisfaction from planning and replanning while not doing the underlying work.

The app should support schedule changes while discouraging repeated major replans. A minimum interval between major changes—perhaps a couple of days or a week—was suggested, but the exact time should be decided later.

## Proposed staged guardrail

- For the first two or three rapid replanning attempts, show a gentle reminder. It may include an encouraging quote or message about consistency and explain that frequent planning can feel like progress without producing actual progress.
- If replanning continues too frequently, the app may temporarily prevent schedule changes for a period.
- The exact frequency threshold, block duration, and which actions are covered by the block will be finalized later.
- Deliberately deferred for later discussion: whether undo and restore actions remain available while the temporary block is active.

## Questions to resolve

- Which actions count as major replanning: rebuilding the full schedule, changing goals, or any approved schedule edit?
- Should undoing a mistake or restoring an old schedule be exempt from a replanning interval?
- Which actions are covered by a temporary block, and should undo/restore actions remain available?
- Should a user have any way to override the block when circumstances genuinely change?
- What interval best reduces planning-for-planning's-sake without preventing useful adaptation?
- How could the app distinguish productive adjustment from a loop of repeated replanning?
