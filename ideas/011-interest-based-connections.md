# Connect People with Shared Interests

**Status:** Exploratory; privacy and product scope are undecided.

## Idea

Use the goals and interests users choose to help them find like-minded people. They could connect to discuss goals, share ways to improve, or potentially meet in real life.

## Questions to resolve

- What user information should be used for matching, and what should remain private?
- Is this peer discovery, direct messaging, communities, events, or something else?
- Should users opt in separately to being discoverable?
- What safety and moderation expectations would apply?
- How does this support the core goal-planning experience?
