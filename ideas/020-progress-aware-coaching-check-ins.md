# Progress-Aware Coaching Check-ins

**Status:** Proposed; timing and intervention details need discussion.

## Idea

Use user-selected check-ins to review how the plan is working and whether the user is making progress. Because the AI knows the scheduled tasks, streaks, and milestones, it can bring up work that is pending or incomplete instead of waiting silently for a phase-end milestone.

The AI should discuss this in a life-coach tone, not as a teacher evaluating a student. It should:

- Mention pending tasks or milestones clearly and without scolding.
- Ask what happened and why the user could not complete the planned work.
- Understand whether the schedule is realistic and working for the user.
- Suggest solutions or changes that could help.
- Reassure and encourage the user without breaking their spirit.

The check-in cadence is user-selected (see [Accountability with Friends](010-social-accountability.md)). The phase-end milestone still triggers the detailed readiness review described in [Phased Goal Journeys](019-phased-goal-journeys.md); progress-aware check-ins can identify stalls before that milestone is reached. Prolonged overdue tasks may trigger app notices, while persistent milestone delays can be discussed during the user's normal check-in. See [Overdue Alerts and Persistent Blockers](021-overdue-alerts-and-persistent-blockers.md).

## Questions to resolve

- How should the AI distinguish a temporary setback from a schedule that consistently does not fit?
- How direct should the AI be when pointing out a pattern of missed work?
- When should it suggest a plan change versus first exploring the cause with the user?
