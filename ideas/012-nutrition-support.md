# Nutrition Aligned with Activity

**Status:** Future possibility; scope and evidence standards need discussion.

## Idea

Eventually help users align nutrition with their activities and energy needs through the day. The product owner's examples include meals around study sessions and post-workout meals.

## Questions to resolve

- Does the user want meal timing, meal ideas, nutrition education, tracking, or personalized plans?
- What user context, if any, should the product ask for before offering nutrition guidance?
- How should the app communicate uncertainty and avoid overclaiming what foods do for focus or performance?
- Should nutrition be kept as a later, separate area until the core planning loop works?
