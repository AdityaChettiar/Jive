# Schedule Around Workload and Energy

**Status:** Proposed; input and scheduling behavior are undecided.

## Idea

Shape tasks around factors such as academic backlog, inconsistent gym attendance, and low energy. Allocate suitable time and duration so work and routines can fit together more realistically.

## Questions to resolve

- How should a student report energy: a quick daily check-in, time-of-day preferences, or another method?
- Should the planner learn from completed, skipped, or shortened tasks?
- How should it estimate task duration and decide when to split a task?
- What should happen when the plan has more work than available time?
