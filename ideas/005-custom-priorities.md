# User Priorities and Changing Contexts

**Status:** Proposed; priority controls need discussion.

## Idea

Make planning highly customizable around the user's priorities. The plan may change when there is extra free time, exams are approaching, the student is on vacation, or another event affects their available time and goals.

## Questions to resolve

- How does a student express priorities: ranked goals, per-activity weights, explicit rules, or a simpler control?
- Should priorities be permanent, date-bounded, or tied to named contexts such as exam week and vacation?
- What should “extra free time” mean, and should the student approve suggested tasks for it?
- How should the planner resolve conflicts between priorities?
