# Accountability with Friends

**Status:** Proposed differentiator; mechanics and design are undecided.

## Idea

The product owner identifies accountability as the differentiating factor that will push users to stay committed and “locked in.” The proposed combination includes:

- Visible streaks for the activities the user chooses.
- Progress photos from time to time.
- Regular individual check-ins.
- Regular check-ins with a small group.
- The user may choose whether progress is visible to their group or public. This is an early preference only; exact choices, defaults, and photo-specific controls are undecided.
- Activity streaks are a common, simple signal of consistency across goals, while milestones and evidence should reflect the specific field or goal.
- Check-ins are also feedback on the plan: users can say what went as planned, what did not, what extra they did, whether the workload was workable or too hectic, and suggest changes. That feedback should improve future AI plans; see [Check-in Feedback and Plan Calibration](013-check-in-feedback-and-plan-calibration.md).
- Keep an always-available place in the app for users to leave feedback whenever they want.
- Let users choose how often the app proactively checks in. Suggested frequency options include daily, every other day, twice weekly, or weekly.
- The always-available feedback interaction should be mostly freeform conversation, with structured prompts as a supplement; see [Conversational Feedback Space](014-conversational-feedback-space.md).
- Proactive check-ins may use the AI's knowledge of progress to discuss pending tasks and milestones, understand what got in the way, and help the user adjust supportively; see [Progress-Aware Coaching Check-ins](020-progress-aware-coaching-check-ins.md).

Accountability should support the full goal-planning loop; it does not replace the need for the planner itself to work well.

## Questions to resolve

- How should a streak count progress for an activity, and how should rest days or a missed day work?
- What visibility choices should users have for streaks, check-ins, and progress photos, and what should the default be?
- How often should individual and group check-ins happen, and are they scheduled or triggered by progress?
- How should the always-available feedback entry point relate to the app's scheduled check-ins?
- What should group members be able to see and encourage?
- How should missed days, rest, and schedule changes affect streaks?
- Is the social layer part of the core product or a later addition?
