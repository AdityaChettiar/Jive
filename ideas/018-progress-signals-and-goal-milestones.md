# Progress Signals and Goal Milestones

**Status:** Proposed progress model; milestone design needs discussion.

## Idea

For goals that are difficult to measure directly, combine several indicators:

- **Consistency:** activity streaks as a common, easy-to-understand signal across goals.
- **Task completion:** evidence of the work done along the way.
- **Goal-specific milestones and artifacts:** tangible signs that skills or outcomes are developing.
- **Self-reported growth:** the user's perception of improvement where it is difficult to measure externally.

Examples of goal-specific evidence include physique updates, coding projects, paintings, language ability, and certifications. Streaks indicate regular effort, while milestones show development or results; one should not be treated as a substitute for the other.

## Agreeing milestones

Milestones should be finalized through conversation. The AI proposes milestones it considers valid for the goal; the user can agree to some, add their own, and discuss them with the AI until they reach an agreed set.

## Questions to resolve

- How should milestones be defined for different kinds of goals?
- How should qualitative progress, such as confidence or skill, be represented?
- Should evidence such as photos, projects, or certificates be private, shareable, or both?
- How can the app celebrate progress without implying that streaks alone equal achievement?
