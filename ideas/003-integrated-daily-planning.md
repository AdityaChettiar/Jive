# One Plan Across Life Areas

**Status:** Proposed; initial scope is undecided.

## Idea

Plan academics alongside personal goals such as gym sessions, diet, daily habits, and content creation, so a student can see how the parts of their day fit together.

## Questions to resolve

- Which of these areas must be supported in the first version?
- Does “diet” mean meal reminders, meal planning, nutrition tracking, or another kind of support?
- Are content creation goals recurring sessions, project milestones, or both?
- Should these activities be tracked as goals, scheduled tasks, or routines?
