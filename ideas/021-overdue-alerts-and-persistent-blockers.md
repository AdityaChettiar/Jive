# Overdue Alerts and Persistent Blockers

**Status:** Notice behavior proposed; deeper intervention strategies deliberately deferred.

## Overdue notices and routine check-ins

- A task that remains unfinished for a prolonged period may trigger an app notice or alert.
- If a task milestone remains incomplete for an extended time, including after repeated schedule restructuring and proposed solutions, the AI may bring it up during the user's routine check-in.
- The check-in should explore what is happening in a supportive, nonjudgmental way, following [Progress-Aware Coaching Check-ins](020-progress-aware-coaching-check-ins.md).

## Strategies to decide later

Two cases need separate product decisions at a later stage:

1. **Inconsistency:** the user is not completing tasks consistently. The right support or intervention is not decided yet.
2. **Consistent effort without milestone progress:** the user is following the plan consistently, but still has not reached the milestone despite repeated schedule restructuring and solutions. A different strategy is needed, but it is not decided yet.

## Questions to resolve later

- How long must a task or milestone remain incomplete before a notice or check-in mention?
- What form should notices take, and how can users control them?
- What intervention should help when inconsistency is the main barrier?
- What should the AI do when the user is consistent but the milestone remains out of reach?
